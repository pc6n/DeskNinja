# v0.2.1 — Todo sorting & due dates

## Highlights

- **Drag to reorder** open todos (manual sort mode)
- **Sort modes** — manual, due date, or created
- **Optional due date & time** per todo, with overdue highlighting
- **Compact todo UI** — flat list, hover actions, completed section

## Download

macOS `.dmg` (Apple Silicon) on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/pc6n/DeskNinja/releases/tag/v0.2.1).

## Notes

- Due dates stored as local timestamps in `~/Library/Application Support/com.deskninja.app/todos.json`
- Existing todos from v0.2.0 are migrated automatically (`sortOrder`, `dueAt`)
